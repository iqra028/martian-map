        Mission briefing

        Marswalk: Sakarya Vallis

        • Terrain is real. Elevation and slope come from a HiRISE stereo model (10 m grid). Legs automatically route around ground steeper than 20° and steer clear of boulder fields seen in the 1 m model.
        • Edit the route. Drag numbered markers or use Add stop. Status, consumables and return times update instantly.
        • Check conditions. Pick a Curiosity weather sol and try dust levels under Environment.
