    Resources used

    • JPL Mars Trek: MOLA elevation (463 m), Viking color mosaic (232 m), THEMIS day IR (100 m)
    • USGS Astrogeology: MRO CTX mosaic (5 m) and the USGS-NASA geologic map of Mars (global, 1:20,000,000, regional context only)
    • NASA Curiosity route map: used to compare our targets with regions NASA scientists labelled on lower Mount Sharp
    • Perseverance raw images: example ground-level views (Jezero Crater, not Gale), captioned per stop
    • NASA Worldview: interface inspiration only (layer toggles, time controls). It holds Earth data.
